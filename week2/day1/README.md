\# Day 1 — Descriptive Statistics



\## Summary

Computed and interpreted the center and spread of the Titanic dataset's `Fare` column, and used the gap between the mean and median to decide which measure better represents a "typical" value.



\## Key Concepts

\- Central tendency: mean, median, mode

\- Spread: range, variance, standard deviation, IQR

\- Percentiles and quartiles

\- Why the mean is pulled toward outliers while the median is not



\## Tasks

\- Loaded the Titanic dataset and inspected its shape, columns, and missing values.

\- Computed the mean, median, mode, standard deviation, and IQR of the `Fare` column.

\- Compared the mean and median to identify that the column is heavily skewed by high-fare outliers.

\- Concluded that the median is the more honest representation of a typical fare, and documented the reasoning in a Markdown cell.



\## Key Takeaway

A large gap between the mean and median is a signal of skewed data — in that case, the median is a more reliable measure of a "typical" value than the mean, since the mean is pulled toward outliers.
